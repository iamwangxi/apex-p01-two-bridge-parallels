# 16. Boundary returns, including the case with no one-sided core

[Contents](../README.md) · [Proof map](main-theorem.md) · [Sources](../sources/bibliography.md)

The next lemma is needed for the middle two-odd type later, which has no disjoint one-sided core. It avoids assuming that absence of such a core permits a half return.

**Lemma 16.1.** Let $`S`$ be any connected, separating, essential two-longitude surface in $`Q`$ of positive genus that occurs in the present assemblies. An oriented return isotopy fixing its two boundary circles individually has meridional winding zero. No returning identity isotopy can exchange the two circles. A returning nested two-boundary system has no boundary permutation and has zero meridional winding.

**Proof of the full-return assertion.** Attach two reaching annuli to $`S`$, fixing an anchor and the relative outer pattern integer $`t`$. A full companion return of winding $`m`$ would identify that one-piece global form with its version of integer $`t+m`$.

Compare these isotopic global forms after putting their companion pieces at two distinct levels of one fixed small collar $`S\times[-\epsilon,\epsilon]`$. Their boundary traces lie in disjoint proper meridional strips $`J_+,J_-`$ on $`T`$. Use empty-product simplification with the efficient $`T`$-tangent pushes from the [geometric foundations](assemblies.md), [whole-block comparison](comparison.md) and [all-even cone](even-cone.md). A horizontal-face portion in $`Q`$ must be the entire $`S`$: no interior boundary can occur, since the companion pieces are disjoint and $`Q`$ has no actual boundary of $`M`$. Cutting a product by its spanning $`T`$ annuli gives products with nonempty horizontal faces. Its $`Q`$ part is therefore an empty product chamber with both entire $`S`$ pieces as horizontal boundary.

Two parallel separating $`S`$ pieces give three chambers. Only the middle slab has both in its frontier. Hence every such $`Q`$ product is that slab in the fixed collar. Each non-final push can leave the moving piece at a nearby parallel level on the other side, still in the collar, without affecting the collapsed intersection curves on the pattern side. A product avoiding $`Q`$ can be pushed away from $`T`$. Finite iteration finishes with the complete surfaces coincident while each companion boundary has stayed in its own original strip. Close the initial and final small radial paths in those strips. The return winding is zero, and the fixed-endpoint pattern-path invariant forces $`t=t+m`$. Thus $`m=0`$. This is the confinement proof of [Lemma 7.3](odd-returns.md); it uses separation and positive genus, and not the existence of a one-sided core.

**Half returns.** A torus identity isotopy preserving the cyclic order of two longitude circles and exchanging them has meridional cyclic displacement $`m+1/2`$. Square that return path, composing with its endpoint map. The resulting oriented return fixes both circles and has odd full displacement $`2m+1`$. The torus map is isotopic to the identity and preserves the meridian direction, so the two displacements add. This contradicts the full-return assertion.

For a nested $`k`$-piece system, a returning cyclic rotation $`r`$ must preserve $`q(i)=-1-i`$, so $`2r=0\bmod 2k`$. The only nonzero possibility is the half rotation $`r=k`$. Its square gives an odd full circuit on every component and is impossible by restriction to any one $`S`$. With $`r=0`$ each component and circle returns individually, and the common meridional winding is zero by the same restriction. ∎

Consequently the $`2^k`$ independent orientations of a fixed ordered system are distinct oriented classes. The relative pattern integer is not erased by a companion return. Longitude spins act trivially on vertical annuli; with outer endpoints fixed and actual endpoints free, the only remaining pattern mapping class is its outer-collar integer. It is detected by the coefficient of $`[b_-]`$ in the positive reaching path, as in [Lemma 6.4](odd-normal-forms.md).

The lemma also justifies using a freely chosen, fixed end marking for a pure stack of a two-boundary type that is not a one-sided frontier. No half motion identifies the two end markings.
