## HT — Hatcher–Thurston

**A. Hatcher and W. Thurston.** *Incompressible surfaces in 2-bridge knot complements*. *Inventiones Mathematicae* **79** (1985), 225–246. Public acquisition route: [Hatcher's author page](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/), papers collection.

Acquisition: supplied public author-copy PDF and extracted text. The mathematical source checks and adversarial review used Theorem 1(b)–(e), p.228; Propositions 1–2 and the Seifert-surface corollary including the fibre clause, p.230; the pillowcase arc classification, p.232; the whole-system proof, pp.233–239; the horizontal regular-neighbourhood frontier and doubled weights, p.240; boundary/Euler counts, pp.243–244; the abstract covering statement, p.244; and component foldings and the exact all-even count, p.245. The branched double-cover lens-space observation is on p.225.

Original page checks in those records included pp.227–228, 230, 232, 240, 243–245. In particular the admissibility condition is absolute coefficient **at least** two, including the endpoint move clause; some extracted inequalities lose the equality sign. The proof here uses the same-sign double moves only at coefficients equal to plus or minus two. This edition is the classification input, not a new classification of the companion's Seifert surfaces.

**Remark (the p.230 aggregate count).** The parenthetical aggregate orbit count on printed p.230 conflicts with the complete moves in Theorem 1(e), p.228. On $`[3,2,4,-2]`$ those moves preserve $`x_1-x_2`$ and give $`2N+1`$ bounded orbits, rather than $`N+1`$. More generally an intervening free block of length $`L`$ gives $`NL+1`$ orbits. [Lemma 14.1](../proof/bounded-parameters.md) proves this directly inside the bounded parameter box. The present proof uses Theorem 1(e) and that bounded argument, and does not use the aggregate aside. Original-page checks for the extended argument also covered printed pp.227, 228, 230, 231, 241, 242, 244 and 245, including Figs.3, 24–28 and 30–31. The independently reproduced p.231 counts for the eight carriers of $`K_{5/13}`$ are $`7,8,4,4,8,8,12,8`$.

## K05 — Kakimizu

**Osamu Kakimizu.** *Classification of the incompressible spanning surfaces for prime knots of 10 or less crossings*. *Hiroshima Mathematical Journal* **35** (2005), 47–92.

Acquisition: supplied public journal PDF and extracted text; public bibliographic route is the journal's 2005 volume 35 archive. Literature comparison: the possibly disconnected, componentwise incompressible convention, p.47; Theorems A and C and geometric IS, pp.48–49; Proposition 2.1, p.56, and Proposition 2.3, p.57; the hypotheses of Theorems 3.12 and 3.15, pp.59, 68, 70–71, and Theorems 5.4 and 5.10, pp.74–75, 78–79; Lemma 6.4 and Figure 6.3, p.84, the nonzero-twist unknotted-core annulus example. The novelty review visually checked pp.47–49 and 84. The small-crossing classification concerns prime knots, and the auxiliary two-component example is not the present zero-framed knotted-core link. The general plumbing tools are not automatically applicable; their minimal-genus starting surfaces have negative Euler characteristic, whereas the target oriented link has a spanning annulus of characteristic zero.
